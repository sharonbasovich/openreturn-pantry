READ BEFORE MAKING A REAL-WORLD DECISION

Useful for asking better questions.
Not a promise of access.

What counts

An origin counts only when a scheduled outbound journey, the full visit within opening hours, and a scheduled return satisfy that origin’s departure, return and time-away limits. Staffed duration stays equal.

What “best” means

The best of the tested start times has the most reachable origins. Ties favor the start closest to current hours, then the earlier start. That is not a count of people or a fairness guarantee. Check both gained and lost origins before choosing a window. A coarser step can miss better windows.

What’s outside the model

No walking, live delays, cancellations, fares, wheelchair access, vehicle capacity or household demand. One supplied service-day timetable, not a GTFS import. Verify schedules and assumptions with operators and the community.
